Why it works

Chemistry-led, ritual-first.

The deck centers the product story around vitamin C for chlorine dechlorination and magnesium as a recovery ingredient. These product claims should be lab-tested and substantiated before launch.

Vitamin C story

The pitch positions ascorbate as the active used to neutralize chlorine on contact. Formulation, dosage and consumer claims need validation.

Indian ingredient cues

Aloe, neem, turmeric and vitamin C create familiar ingredient language for an India-first personal care brand.

Built for frequency

The opportunity is not a one-off treatment. It is a repeatable ritual around every swim session.
